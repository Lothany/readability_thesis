Let's Learn About:
Mutualism Symbiosis
Neo, in this story, is a sea anemone. The small
bright colored fish which approaches Neo is
a clown fish. These two sea creatures have a
special relationship with one another.
Sea anemones have poisonous tentacles that
clown fishes are immune to because of a
certain slime on their body. Because the sea
anemone's tentacles deter predators from

28


approaching, clown fishes often hide there to
stay protected.
Meanwhile, clown fishes help sea anemones
by eating the parasitic algae that grow on
their bodies.
This relationship is called mutualism
symbiosis because of the mutual benefits on
both sides.
